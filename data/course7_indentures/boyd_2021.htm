“Additional Assets” means:

(1)            any Property (other than cash, cash equivalents or securities) to be owned (or, in the case of any Property subject to the Master Lease or an Additional Lease, leased) by the Company or a Restricted Subsidiary and used in a Core Business or a Related Business;
