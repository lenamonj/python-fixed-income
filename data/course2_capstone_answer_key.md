# Answer key: course2_capstone_holdings.csv

`course2_capstone_holdings.csv` is the desk's book as of 2026-11-30, with these bond-math problems planted on purpose. Generated by `tools/make_course2_data.py` with seed 20261130. The clean book has 199 bonds and the file has 201 rows. No row carries two problems. Settlement is the as-of date for every bond; the conventions are semiannual US 30/360 as in `COURSE2_SPEC.md`. In every row with a planted problem, every other column holds its clean value, and `market_value` is the clean value in every row.

## Dirty price in the price column (3 rows)

- `99094QAD3` (NEVE): in the file `108.955`, clean value `107.601`
- `99093PAA2` (TROE): in the file `101.276`, clean value `99.404`
- `99085HAA0` (TROR): in the file `99.839`, clean value `99.056`

## Market value that does not tie out (6 rows)
Not a separate problem. In the 3 dirty-price rows and the 3 actual/360 accrued rows, `market_value` does not equal `par_held` x (`price` + `accrued`) / 100. In the 2 blank-price rows it cannot be checked until the price is filled. It ties out in every row once the fixes are made.

## Clean on purpose
These two bonds are new this month. They are correct, and a reprice that follows Excel's conventions reproduces them. Neither is in `holdings.csv` or `benchmark.csv`.
- `99008IYA6` (OSMC, Osmere Chemicals), 5.5 percent maturing 2033-08-31, maturing on a month end.
- `99007HYA9` (TRNW, Tarnwick Utilities), 4.25 percent maturing 2027-03-15, in its final coupon period.

`99007HYA9` has one coupon left, so `PRICE` uses simple interest and `YIELD` its closed form. `99008IYA6` matures on a month end, so every coupon falls on a month end (previous coupon 2026-08-31, next 2027-02-28). Coupon dates stepped from the previous date instead of from maturity drift to the 28th after February and give the wrong accrued.
